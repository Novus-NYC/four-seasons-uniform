## 4. Visual content and branding

All of this section is **[Observation]** unless a source is named.

### Storefront

- **Current awning** (Google hero photo). Royal blue awning. "Four Seasons" in white with a yellow F and an orange-red S, "Uniform(s)" in green beneath. A thin white rule separates the name from brand names (CHEROKEE, Barco uniforms, Dickies logo) and the address "73-19 Roosevelt Ave." in yellow. The side panel shows the end of "Grey's Anatomy." Glass storefront below with headless mannequins in navy, teal, olive and royal blue scrubs, a green Cherokee neon sign, a "META labwear" poster, and a tall poster of a smiling woman in a magenta scrub set.
- **Older awning** (Google user photo). Same royal blue. "Four Seasons Fashion" with colored initials (yellow F, orange S, yellow F), "Uniforms" in yellow-green, "Grey's Anatomy" in yellow, "Nurse Mates," the phone (718) 672-4004, plus Cherokee, Barco and Dickies logos. Rolling security gate down. Neon window sign reading "Uniforms & Shoes" next to a neon nurse figure.
- **Facebook photos** show a wider street view where the awning runs across a broad storefront with a separate "Boutique" sign above, and a close-up of the awning lit in sun.
- **Setting**. Under the elevated 7 train's steel structure, busy sidewalk, yellow awning of a neighboring shop (Google and Yelp photos).

### Interior

- Drop ceiling with fluorescent panels, packed racks and walls.
- Yellow slatwall with scrub tops on mannequin busts (floral print, hunter green, ceil blue with navy panels, royal blue).
- Shoes and clogs bagged in clear plastic on wall shelves, white leather work shoes displayed at the counter.
- Brand posters, including a man in a white lab coat.
- Counter covered with handwritten and printed signs (store policy, special orders, camera notice, bag notice).
